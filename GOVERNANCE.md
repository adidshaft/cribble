# Cribble Governance

Cribble is an owner-led open-source project. Contributions are welcome, but
product direction, roadmap, official releases, distribution, monetization, and
brand use are controlled by the maintainer.

## Maintainer

The current maintainer and product decision owner is Aman Pandey.

Contact: `adidshaft@kyokasuigetsu.xyz`

## Decision model

The maintainer has final say on:

- product direction and roadmap;
- user experience and design direction;
- local-first, reader-first, and preview-before-mutation policy;
- official release timing and release notes;
- App Store, DMG, signing, notarization, and update channels;
- monetization and paid distribution;
- use of the Cribble name, logo, and official branding.

Accepted pull requests may influence the product, but merging a change does not
create an obligation to accept related future changes.

## Contribution model

Direct pull requests are welcome for focused bugs, docs, tests, accessibility
fixes, rendering improvements, diagnostics, and agreed-upon provider or
integration work.

Feature proposals, major UX changes, storage/write behavior, AI behavior,
monetization, and roadmap changes should start as an issue or GitHub Discussion
before implementation.

## Official releases

Only maintainer-approved builds published through the official GitHub releases,
website, Sparkle feed, or App Store channels are official Cribble releases.
Forks and modified builds are allowed under the project license, but they should
not present themselves as official Cribble builds.

## Brand and trademark

The Apache-2.0 license covers the source code, not the Cribble name, app icon,
logo, screenshots, or other brand assets.

You may:

- refer to Cribble truthfully when describing compatibility, forks, or
  derivative work;
- keep license and attribution notices;
- use screenshots for commentary, reviews, or documentation.

You may not, without maintainer permission:

- distribute a modified app as "Cribble";
- use the Cribble logo or icon as the primary branding for a fork;
- imply that a fork, package, website, or release is official;
- use Cribble branding in a way that confuses users about source or support.

## If maintainership changes

If the maintainer steps away, the preferred path is to appoint a trusted
maintainer or clearly archive the project. Until that happens, the existing
license remains in effect, but official product direction and branding do not
automatically transfer.
